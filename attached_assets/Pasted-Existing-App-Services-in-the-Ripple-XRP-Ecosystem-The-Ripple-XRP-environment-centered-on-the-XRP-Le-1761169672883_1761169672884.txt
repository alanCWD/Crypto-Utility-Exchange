Existing App Services in the Ripple/XRP Ecosystem
The Ripple/XRP environment, centered on the XRP Ledger (XRPL), has evolved significantly by October 2025 into a robust ecosystem focused on payments, tokenization, and institutional finance. Key app services include:
	•	Cross-Border Payment Platforms: RippleNet and On-Demand Liquidity (ODL) enable instant settlements using XRP as a bridge asset, integrated with partners like Bitso, Tranglo, and MoonPay.
	•	Wallets and DEX Interfaces: Tools like Xumm (mobile wallet) and XRPL DEX for trading XRP and tokens.
	•	Custody and Tokenization Services: Ripple Custody, acquired Metaco, and partnerships with Archax for real-world asset (RWA) tokenization (e.g., gold, T-bills).
	•	Stablecoin Issuance: Ripple USD (RLUSD), launched in late 2024, available on exchanges like Uphold and Bitstamp for stable transactions.
	•	DeFi Primitives: Automated Market Makers (AMMs), Hooks for lightweight smart contracts, and institutional lending protocols (undercollateralized loans integrating RLUSD and RWAs, rolled out in Q3 2025).
	•	EVM-Compatible dApps: The XRPL EVM sidechain, live since June 2025, supports Ethereum-based decentralized applications (dApps) with low-cost access to XRPL’s 6+ million wallets.
	•	Liquidity Management: Liquidity Hub for bridging crypto-fiat gaps, and the $1B Digital Asset Treasury (DAT) initiative via GTreasury acquisition for corporate treasury operations.
	•	NFT and Creator Tools: $250M Creator Fund for NFT minting on XRPL, with marketplaces like Sologenic.
These services emphasize enterprise-grade efficiency, with XRPL processing 1,500+ TPS at fractions of a cent per transaction, and over $1.5T in value moved since 2012.
Notable Gap: Consumer-Facing Decentralized Exchanges (CEX-Like Apps)
One prominent app service that does not yet exist in the Ripple/XRP environment is a consumer-oriented, centralized exchange (CEX)-style app—a user-friendly mobile/desktop platform offering spot trading, margin trading, fiat on-ramps, and staking rewards tailored for retail users holding XRP or XRPL tokens.
While XRPL has a built-in decentralized exchange (DEX) for order-book trading and AMMs for liquidity pools, it lacks a polished, all-in-one CEX app like Binance or Coinbase that simplifies fiat-to-XRP conversions, advanced trading pairs (e.g., XRP/USDT perpetuals), and gamified rewards programs for everyday users. Current options like Uphold or Bitso are general-purpose exchanges supporting XRP but not native to the XRPL ecosystem, missing deep integrations like one-click RLUSD swaps or EVM sidechain bridging in a single app.
This gap persists due to Ripple’s institutional focus (e.g., banks via RippleNet) and past regulatory hurdles, which delayed retail tools until the SEC case resolved in May 2025. Community discussions highlight the need for such an app to boost adoption, but as of now, no dedicated XRPL-native CEX app has launched—Evmos and Coreum bridges help interoperability but don’t fill this retail trading void. Future developments, like expanded EVM dApps, may address it, but it remains absent today.
